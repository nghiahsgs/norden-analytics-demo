Norden Analytics AB

Data infrastructure for Nordic energy markets.

About us

Norden Analytics AB (org.nr 559112-4471) is a Swedish data company founded in 2021 and headquartered in Stockholm. The company operates a market-data platform covering Nord Pool spot prices, grid frequency telemetry, and district-heating demand forecasts for municipal energy utilities in Sweden, Norway and Finland.

Products

  • Norden Spot Feed — real-time and historical electricity price data via REST API.
  • GridSense — frequency and congestion analytics for regional grid operators.
  • Värmeplan — district-heating demand forecasting with hourly resolution.

Team & offices

The company employs 34 people across offices in Stockholm (HQ, Södermalm) and Gävle (data operations). Engineering works primarily in Python, Rust and TypeScript, with infrastructure hosted in Swedish data centres.
